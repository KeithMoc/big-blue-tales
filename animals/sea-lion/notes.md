# California sea lion: modelling notes and review record

Species: California sea lion, Zalophus californianus. Units: 1 model unit = 75 cm; the body is 3 units (2.25 m)
from the nose to the tip of the tail, and the hind flippers reach about 0.25 units further back.
Head at +x, tail at -x. We model a grown male, because the dossier describes the male's crest, size and colours
in most detail (NOAA, NPS, Smithsonian, The Marine Mammal Center). The fact dossier is `dossier.json`.

## Counts and proportions checked against sources

| Feature | In life (source) | In the model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Length, grown male | about 7.5 ft / 2.3 m (NOAA); 7 ft (TMMC); almost 8 ft (NPS) | 2.25 m nose to tail |
| Body shape | "streamlined, torpedo-shaped bodies taper to the tail", "strong, bulky shoulders" (Smithsonian) | deepest at the shoulders and chest, long taper to the hips |
| Head | "dog-like head" (Smithsonian); "muzzle is dog-like and long, slightly tapering to a moderately blunt end" (NPS) | long muzzle, blunt nose pad |
| Crest | grown males: "high, domed forehead" (Smithsonian), "raised forehead" (NPS), lighter with age (NPS, NOAA) | raised forehead, painted a lighter tan |
| Ear flaps | "visible ear pinnae (ear flaps) on either side of its head" (NPS), small (Smithsonian) | two small pointed flaps behind the eyes |
| Whiskers | "sensitive whiskers, called vibrissae" (Smithsonian) | 14 pale whiskers on each side of the muzzle |
| Front flippers | "especially long" (Smithsonian), "large and winglike" (NPS), black (NPS) | long, wing-like, black, beat up and down |
| Hind flippers | used as rudders (Smithsonian, NOAA); black (NPS) | broad, black, trailing behind the tail |
| Tail | "small flattened tail between the hind flippers" (NPS) | the body tapers to a small tail between them |
| Colour, male | dark brown to black (NOAA); dark chestnut, some lighter on the sides and belly (Smithsonian, NPS) | dark chestnut back, sandier belly |
| Swimming | thrust from the front flippers; hind flippers steer (NOAA, Feldkamp 1987, SeaWorld) | only the front flippers beat; the back body flexes a little |

## Known departures from life

- The height and length of the porpoising leaps are our guess. No source gives them. The caption says so.
- The porpoise action shows two leaps; the sources say only that sea lions are "sometimes seen porpoising".
- The exact place and shape of the ear flaps, the number and length of the whiskers and the size of the eyes
  are judgements from the words "small", "visible" and "dog-like"; no source gives measurements.
- The hind flippers are one smooth paddle each; real ones have separate toes and small claws (three, or three to
  five, by source) that we do not show.
- The mouth stays shut.
- In life the crest's light colour varies a lot from male to male (NPS: "highly variable").

## Open questions

- Top swimming speed differs by source (25 to 30 mph, 25 mph, 13.4 mph). Not used in the text.
- Deepest dive: 274 m (NPS, SeaWorld) against a 400 m lower depth limit (IUCN). The depth chart uses 274 m.
- Animal Diversity Web says they echolocate; no agency source opened here says so. Left out.
